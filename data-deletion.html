Data Deletion Instructions

HeadGeek81 ClipBot. Last updated October 29, 2026.

ClipBot does not collect, store or sell personal data about visitors or any other people. It is a private tool used by its owner to post her own stream clips to her own accounts. The only information it keeps is the owner's own clip titles, post dates and view counts.

To remove access

If you connected your Facebook or Instagram account to this app and want to remove it:

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings, then Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  2. Find HeadGeek ClipBot and click Remove.
  3. Choose to delete any data it posted, if asked.

This ends the app's access to your account right away.

To ask for deletion

You can also ask for any related data to be deleted by contacting the owner through twitch.tv/headgeek81. Requests are handled promptly.
