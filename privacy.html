Privacy Policy

HeadGeek81 ClipBot. Last updated October 22, 2026.

ClipBot is a private tool built and used by one person, the streamer HeadGeek81, to share her own Twitch stream clips to her own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts. It is not a public service and no one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, ClipBot connects to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It uses that access only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos, read the view and like counts of those posts, and delete posts the owner scheduled.

What it collects

ClipBot does not collect, store or share personal information about visitors or any other people. The public dashboard shows only the owner's own clip titles, post dates and view counts.

Where login tokens live

The owner's access tokens are kept in encrypted GitHub Secrets and are never published or shared with anyone.

Sharing and selling data

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Google user data

ClipBot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
